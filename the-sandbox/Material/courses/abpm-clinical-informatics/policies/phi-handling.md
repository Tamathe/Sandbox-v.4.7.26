# PHI Handling Policy

This is a clinical informatics course. We talk about clinical cases constantly. We never put real Protected Health Information into this platform. Not in discussions, not in study groups, not in capstone writeups, not in voice sessions, not anywhere. The platform is not a HIPAA-covered environment and we are not pretending it is.

## What counts as PHI

The HIPAA 18 identifiers — names, dates more granular than year, geographic subdivisions smaller than state, medical record numbers, device IDs, biometric identifiers, photographs, and the rest of the list. If you're not sure whether something is an identifier, it is.

## How to talk about real cases without putting PHI here

- **Abstract before you type.** "A 60-year-old with sepsis on the medical floor" is not PHI. "The patient I admitted last Tuesday from clinic" is on its way to being PHI even if you change the age.
- **Strip dates.** Use intervals, not calendars. "POD 2" not "March 14."
- **Strip rarities.** A condition rare enough that one case in your hospital identifies the patient should be generalized to a more common analogue.
- **Strip institution names** when the combination of institution + condition is identifying.
- **When in doubt, invent the case.** A composite or hypothetical case teaches the same lesson and carries no risk.

## What is explicitly prohibited

- Pasting screenshots from any EHR, real or test, that contain identifiers.
- Uploading reports, notes, lab values, or images tied to real patients.
- Naming colleagues, patients, or institutions in a way that would let a third party identify a case.
- Asking the AI tutor to "use a real example from your training data" — that is not how the model works, and the request itself signals a misunderstanding worth correcting.

## What to do if PHI is posted by accident

1. Edit or delete the post immediately.
2. Notify the instructor through the platform's normal channels.
3. The instructor will purge any cached copies and document the correction. There is no penalty for an honest report; there is a serious one for trying to hide it.

## Why this policy is gated

This policy must be acknowledged before any case-based discussion thread or study-group conversation unlocks. Case discussion is where PHI accidents happen, so the gate sits there.

## Acknowledgment

By acknowledging this policy you are saying: *I will treat this platform as the public space it is, and I will keep real patients out of it.*
