# Reading List

A running list. None of it is required. All of it is useful. Read what scratches the itch a given module raised. The first three books are the closest things this field has to a shared canon, and the ABPM item writers have read them.

## Books

- **Biomedical Informatics: Computer Applications in Health Care and Biomedicine** — Shortliffe & Cimino. The core textbook. Long, dense, worth it. If you read one book for this exam, this is the one.
- **Clinical Informatics Study Guide** — Finnell & Dixon. Written explicitly for board candidates. Maps tightly to the ABPM content outline.
- **Health Informatics: Practical Guide** — Hoyt & Hersh. Lighter than Shortliffe, broader than the study guide. Good entry point.
- **The Digital Doctor** — Robert Wachter. The honest book about what computerization did to medicine. You will be asked about sociotechnical failure modes; this book *is* those failure modes.
- **To Err Is Human / Crossing the Quality Chasm** — IOM. The two reports the entire patient-safety movement traces back to. Skim them; know what they argue.

## Foundational papers and reports

- **Friedman's "Fundamental Theorem of Biomedical Informatics"** — one page, gets cited everywhere, know it cold.
- **The CDS Five Rights** — Osheroff et al. The framing every CDS question on the boards is built on.
- **Sociotechnical model for studying health IT** — Sittig & Singh, 2010. The eight-dimension model. Memorize the dimensions.
- **SAFER Guides** — ONC. Self-assessment guides for EHR safety. Skim each one.
- **AMIA Clinical Informatics core competencies** — the syllabus the field agreed on. The ABPM outline tracks it.

## Standards references (read enough to recognize, not memorize)

- **HL7 FHIR specification** — hl7.org/fhir. Read the overview, the resource list, and one resource (Patient or Observation) end to end.
- **HL7 v2 message structure** — any reference. Know ADT, ORM, ORU, MDM segments at a glance.
- **SNOMED CT, LOINC, RxNorm** — know what each is *for*, what granularity it operates at, and which one wins for which use case.

## Policy and law

- **HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules** — HHS summary pages. Know the categories of safeguards.
- **HITECH Act** — what it added on top of HIPAA, especially breach notification and Meaningful Use.
- **21st Century Cures Act + ONC information blocking rule** — the eight exceptions. The boards love these.

## Long-form to follow

- **JAMIA** (Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association)
- **Applied Clinical Informatics**
- Wachter's writing on health IT
- ONC and CMS rule announcements — dry but load-bearing

## Question banks (complement, not substitute)

- **Board Vitals — Clinical Informatics**
- **AMIA review course question bank**
- The official **ABPM sample questions** — short but calibrated to the real item style. Read the rationales twice.
