# AI Use Policy

AI tutoring is part of how this course is built. Used well, a model is the cheapest patient simulator and the most patient explainer you will ever have. Used badly, it is a way to feel like you studied without having studied. This policy exists to keep you on the first side of that line.

## Where AI helps and is encouraged

- **Explaining a concept three different ways** until one lands. Models are excellent at this.
- **Generating practice questions** in the style of the boards, especially around standards (FHIR, SNOMED, HL7) where drilling matters.
- **Quizzing you on the wrong-answer rationales** in the mastery gates. Have the model ask "*why* is option C wrong?" and force yourself to answer first.
- **Roleplaying a skeptical attending** during voice-defense capstones. That is the entire point of the voice sessions.
- **Translating between vocabularies**: e.g., "explain Arden Syntax in terms a SQL person would recognize."

## Where AI is not allowed

- **Writing your capstones.** Not the first draft, not the outline, not the closing paragraph. The capstones are the part where the learning is locked in by the act of writing. Outsourcing the writing outsources the learning.
- **Answering mastery-gate questions for you.** The gate is a self-test. Cheating yourself on a self-test is not even a moral question, it's a logical one.
- **Generating fake citations.** If a model gives you a citation, verify it. If you can't verify it, don't use it.
- **Producing PHI substitutes.** Do not ask a model to "make up a realistic patient." The illusion of realism in case discussions is a trap; we use abstractions on purpose. See the PHI Handling Policy.

## How to cite AI use

If a model's words made it into your writeup, mark them. A single inline note is enough:
*"(Phrasing of this sentence borrowed from a Claude conversation, 2026-04-06.)"*
You don't need a formal citation format. You need honesty.

## Acknowledgment

By acknowledging this policy you are saying: *I will use AI to learn faster, not to learn less.*
